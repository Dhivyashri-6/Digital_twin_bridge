# Limitations and future work

Z24 is a benchmark; historical replay is not live deployment. BOCPD detects statistical changes rather than damage. SHAP is attribution rather than causation. Sensor importance can prioritize inspection but is not definitive localization. Coordinates may be schematic. The risk score is not a structural safety index or certification.

Future work includes live IoT ingestion, cloud/edge monitoring, a physics-based twin, stronger localization, LSTM and Transformer experiments, sensor fusion, mobile delivery, predictive maintenance, and validation on an operating bridge.
